# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Chinese-speaking investors and builders who follow US equities, especially AI infrastructure, semiconductors, cloud, software, and high-volatility growth stocks. They use the product at a desk during market prep, after hours, or while reading news and want a fast way to turn a ticker into a research queue.

## Product Purpose

This product is a local US stock research dashboard. It shows live-ish market data, price trend context, a watchlist, and a Serenity-inspired research score that separates market momentum from evidence quality, supply-chain bottleneck proximity, and risk checks. Success means the user can quickly decide what to research next, what evidence is still missing, and what would falsify a thesis.

## Brand Personality

Calm, evidence-first, focused. The interface should feel like a serious research terminal translated into a clear consumer-grade dashboard, not a trading game.

## Anti-references

Avoid meme-stock casino styling, broker-homepage marketing, heavy dark navy/gold finance tropes, decorative AI gradients, oversized hero sections, and any UI that implies guaranteed returns or personalized financial advice.

## Design Principles

- Separate signal from decision: show research prompts and risk gates, never tell the user to buy or sell.
- Make uncertainty visible: distinguish observed market data from heuristic scores and assumptions.
- Keep the workflow dense but calm: prioritize scanning, comparison, and repeated use.
- Use Serenity as a method: start from demand, bottlenecks, evidence, valuation pressure, and falsification.
- Work locally first: no account, no API key, minimal setup.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-operable controls, visible focus states, colorblind-safe status encoding with text labels, and reduced-motion alternatives.
